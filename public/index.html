What is the Gemini watermark?

The Gemini watermark is the four-point sparkle logo Google stamps in the corner of every image and video its AI models generate, Gemini, Veo, Imagen and Nano Banana. Alongside the visible mark, Google embeds an invisible pattern called SynthID, baked into the pixels themselves.

According to Google DeepMind, SynthID is designed to survive cropping and compression, which is why screenshotting just the corner does not fully clear it. Google applies the logo to every output by default and gives you no setting to disable it, so removing it afterward is the only route to a clean file.

Free metadata tools

Four different jobs. This band is the metadata one.

Metadata is information stored alongside an image, video, or PDF. An empty metadata scan does not prove a person made the picture, and it does not remove a label a platform adds after you publish.

  • Visible watermark

    The Gemini or Veo mark you can see. The remover at the top of this page handles that.

  • Metadata

    Camera, GPS, editing, and Content Credentials stored beside the file. The new tools read or clear those records.

  • SynthID

    An invisible pattern in the pixels. It is a separate tool, and a metadata scan does not remove it.

  • Humanizer

    Changes how the picture looks. Metadata cleaning leaves the visible picture and does not humanize it.
